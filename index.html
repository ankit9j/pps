Synthesizing Leading Global Early-Learning Frameworks

Grounded in the best pedagogical research from Reggio Emilia, Maria Montessori, and Howard Gardner.

Framework 01

Reggio Emilia ("The Studio of Curiosity")

Viewing the child as a curious, capable researcher. We value the process of creation over the final product.

Framework 02

Montessori Exploration

Self-directed sensory and cognitive toolsets guided by trained early-learning facilitators.

Framework 03

Howard Gardner’s Multiple Intelligences

Featuring our Intrapersonal Haven — a sanctuary honoring internal emotional self-regulation and spatial discovery.

Framework 04

Sensory Integration & Loose Parts

Open-ended play environments using natural materials to build motor skills, body awareness, and problem-solving without screens.
